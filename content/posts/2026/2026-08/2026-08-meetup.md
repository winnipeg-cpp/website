## Winnipeg C++ Developers - August 2026

![Winnipeg C++ Developers Meetup](/images/event.jpeg)

Join Winnipeg C++ Developers for a discussion of more advanced CMake uses. The discussion will
ultimately arrive at test discovery in a custom unit test framework.

### Event Details

**Date:** Thursday, August 27, 2026  
**Time:** 5:45 PM - 7:45 PM CDT  
**Location:** Bill and Helen Norrie Library  
15 Poseidon Bay, Winnipeg, MB R3M 3E4

### What to Expect

This month, we're talking about some more advanced uses of CMake, ultimately arriving at test
discovery in a custom unit test framework. Anything is possible, and let's find out how!

This is open to those who are interested in the C++ programming language, whether you work with
it professionally or as a hobby. We will begin on topic, but we will be free to diverge, even to
programming languages other than C++.
