## Winnipeg C++ Developers - July 2026 Meetup

![Winnipeg C++ Developers Meetup](/images/event.jpeg)

Join Winnipeg C++ Developers for an introductory discussion of CMake. Whether you work with C++
professionally or explore it as a hobby, you are welcome to take part.

### Event Details

**Date:** Monday, July 27, 2026  
**Time:** 5:45 PM - 7:45 PM CDT  
**Location:** Bill and Helen Norrie Library  
15 Poseidon Bay, Winnipeg, MB R3M 3E4

### What to Expect

We will discuss what CMake is, how to use it at a basic level, and why developers should use it.
The meetup is hosted by Braden Ganetsky.

We will begin with C++, but the discussion may diverge into other programming languages.

### Registration

### Getting There

The group will meet in the program room at the back of the library.

We look forward to seeing you there!
